Technical leadership

Prof. Kristopher Micinski (PhD, Computer Science)

Deep technical judgment, applied to real business.

For more than a decade, Dr. Micinski’s work has spanned artificial intelligence, programming languages, reverse engineering, and software security. He is a tenured professor at Syracuse University, where his research group develops new techniques for analyzing complex software and data systems.

As technical lead at Superior Intelligence, he brings that experience to complex business problems—understanding how the work actually happens, identifying what is worth solving, and building systems that hold up in practice.
